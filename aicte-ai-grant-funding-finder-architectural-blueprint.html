Hybrid Match Scoring Breakdown

Rule-based score (max 100) feeds the 40% component. IBM Granite AI score (0–100) feeds the 60% component. Final = (rule × 0.4) + (AI × 0.6). AI is only called when rule-based ≥ 30.

Domain match
30 pts
Stage match
25 pts
Location match
20 pts
DPIIT recognition bonus
10 pts
Keyword overlap (up to 3)
15 pts
